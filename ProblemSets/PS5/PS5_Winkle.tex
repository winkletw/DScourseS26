\subsection*{Scraping}

\noindent This data tracks grant awards from the Texas Space Commission. I thought it would be interesting to pair with federal data and estimate the effects of public R/D on a variety of outcomes similar to the Embrapa seminar last fall (Ackerman et al. 2026). I followed Grant McDermott's SelectorGadget notes.
